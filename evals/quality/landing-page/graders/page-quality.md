FAIL if any of these clear problems is present; otherwise PASS.
- The headline doesn't say what Tally does or what the reader gets, in plain words (a slogan with no meaning, or a question).
- The page invents customers, numbers, logos or quotes beyond the two customers and figures given.

- There's more than one main call to action competing for attention, or the only call to action is "Learn more".
- It reads like a brochure: stacked adjectives, vague superlatives ("the best", "the ultimate"), or exclamation marks in most sections.
