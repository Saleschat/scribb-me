This checks for over-correction: writing that avoids AI habits so hard it turns stilted.
PASS if the prose reads naturally: sentence length varies, punctuation is ordinary (a dash or semicolon where it fits is fine), and nothing sounds robotic, clipped or oddly worded, as if words were avoided on purpose.
FAIL if most sentences are the same length and shape, the text reads like a telegram, or phrasing is awkward in a way a human editor would fix.
