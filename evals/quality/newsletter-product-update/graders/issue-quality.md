FAIL if any of these clear problems is present; otherwise PASS.
- The subject line is clickbait or hype ("You won't believe…", "Big news!", all caps) rather than saying what's new.
- It opens with pleasantries or throat-clearing before getting to the feature.
- It covers other news or features besides scheduled reports.
- It reads like a press release: stacked adjectives, "revolutionary", "game-changing", exclamation marks in most paragraphs.
- It ends with a recap that repeats the issue.
