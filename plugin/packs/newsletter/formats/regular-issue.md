**Subject:** What we learned from 40 pricing pages

**Preview:** Three patterns that made plans easier to compare.

Hi there,

This month I read 40 SaaS pricing pages and timed how long it took to find the cheapest plan that fit a five-person team. The fastest pages had three things in common: a monthly price shown first, plan names that describe who they're for, and a single comparison table.
