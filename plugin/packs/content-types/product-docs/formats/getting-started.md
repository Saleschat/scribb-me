# Get started with Foo

Connect your first project and see its events on a dashboard. It takes about five minutes.

## Steps
1. Sign up at foo.example.com and create a workspace.
2. Select **New project**, enter a name, and select **Create**.
3. Copy the snippet on the **Install** page into your site's `<head>`.
4. Visit your site, then open **Events** in Foo.
