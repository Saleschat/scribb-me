## Your situation
Your finance team spends five working days each month building the board report from four systems. Last quarter it went out two days late.

## What we'll do
Connect your finance, payroll, warehouse and CRM systems to one reporting model, and rebuild the board report on it. Out of scope: changes to the systems themselves.

## Outcomes
- The draft report is ready on the first working day of each month, measured over the pilot's two month-ends.
- Every figure links to its source system.

## Timeline
Weeks 1–2: connect the systems. Weeks 3–4: rebuild the report. Weeks 5–6: run two month-ends side by side.

## Price
£18,000 for the six-week pilot, invoiced in two halves.

## Next step
Sign by Friday 10 October to start on 14 October.
