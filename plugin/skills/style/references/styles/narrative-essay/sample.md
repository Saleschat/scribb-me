**Subject:** Scheduled reports are here

**Preview:** The Monday screenshot can stop. Foo sends the dashboard for you.

Hi there,

Every Monday morning, someone opens a dashboard, takes a screenshot and pastes it into Slack. Many of you told us that someone is you.

From today, Foo can do it for you. You can schedule a report from any dashboard: pick the people, the day and the time, and Foo emails them a PDF of the dashboard with the latest numbers.

The screenshot itself only takes a minute. What bothered me is how fast it goes stale. By Thursday, the chart in the channel shows Monday's numbers, and it looks just as sure of itself as it did when it was posted. A scheduled report is built from live data each time it goes out, so the numbers in the inbox are the numbers in Foo.

To set one up, open a dashboard and select **Share** > **Schedule report**. Reports are free on every plan during October.

[Read how scheduled reports work](https://example.com/docs/scheduled-reports)

Next Monday, nobody has to take the screenshot.

Thanks for reading,
Maya, Foo's product lead
