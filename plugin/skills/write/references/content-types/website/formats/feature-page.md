# Receipts find their own transactions

Matching receipts to card payments eats an afternoon every month.

Forward a receipt to receipts@yourcompany.ledgerly.com. Ledgerly reads the amount and date, finds the payment and attaches the receipt. Anything it can't match waits in a short list for you.

- Works with PDF, photos and email bodies
- Matches within 3 days of the payment date

[Turn on receipt matching](https://example.com/receipts)
