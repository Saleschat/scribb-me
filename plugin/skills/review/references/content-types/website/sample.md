# Close the month in two days

Ledgerly matches your bank feeds to your books every night, so month-end takes a short review.

Most finance teams spend the first week of every month chasing receipts and reconciling the same transactions by hand. The numbers are late, and nobody trusts them until they've been checked twice.

## Your books match your bank every morning
Ledgerly reconciles each transaction overnight and flags only the ones it can't match.

## Receipts find their own transactions
Forward a receipt by email and it attaches itself to the right payment.

## Month-end becomes a review
You open a short list of exceptions instead of a spreadsheet of thousands of rows.
