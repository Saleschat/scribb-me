**Slide 1. Board reports used to be a week of copy and paste.** Four systems, one spreadsheet, five working days. That worked when the board met quarterly; it meets monthly now.

**Slide 2. Late numbers cost you the meeting.** Last quarter the report went out two days late, and the forecast discussion got cut.

**Slide 3. The report is ready on day one.** Your analysts check numbers instead of assembling them, and spend the week on the forecast.

**Slide 4. Four systems don't talk to each other.** Every month someone reconnects them by hand.

**Slide 5. Fieldnote connects them once.** Finance, payroll, warehouse and CRM feed one model, refreshed nightly.

**Slide 8. Northwind Freight cut its board report from five days to one.** Same size, same four systems, first quarter.

**Slide 9. A six-week pilot, £18,000.** Next step: a call with your systems lead on Thursday 16 October.
