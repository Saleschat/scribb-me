# Board reports on day one, for Harbour Logistics

**Prepared for Dana Whitfield, Finance Director, Harbour Logistics**

Your team rebuilds the monthly board report by hand from four systems. It takes five working days, and last quarter it went out two days late.

Fieldnote Analytics connects those four systems once and builds the report for you. Your team checks the numbers instead of assembling them.

## What changes
- The draft report is ready on the first working day of the month.
- Every figure links back to its source, so questions get answered in the meeting.
- Your analysts spend that week on the forecast, which you said gets squeezed today.

## Why now
Your new warehouse system goes live in January. Connecting it once, at launch, avoids adding a fifth manual step.

## Proof
Northwind Freight, a carrier of similar size, cut its board report from five days to one in the first quarter.

A six-week pilot for the finance team costs £18,000. If it works, the annual licence is £42,000.
