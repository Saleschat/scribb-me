# Northwind closes its books in two days

Northwind Supplies is a 60-person wholesaler with accounts in three countries.

Their finance team of four spent ten working days on each month-end, mostly matching transactions by hand.

They connected their bank feeds to Ledgerly and set matching rules for their five biggest suppliers.

Month-end now takes two days. Exceptions dropped from about 900 a month to 40.

"I get the first week of the month back." Priya Nair, Finance Lead
