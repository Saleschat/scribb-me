Use one PostHog project for scribb, separate from Saleschat's product analytics.

- The plugin's events are anonymous, and Saleschat's are tied to people. Keeping them apart keeps that promise easy to check.
- If scribb moves to its own organization, you can hand over the project without touching Saleschat's data.

A new project in Saleschat's existing organization also works, but it's harder to hand over later.
