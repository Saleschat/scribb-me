**Subject:** Scheduled reports are here

**Preview:** Send any dashboard to your team every Monday, without opening the app.

Hi there,

You can now schedule a report from any dashboard. Pick the people, the day and the time, and Foo emails them a PDF of the dashboard with the latest numbers.

We built this because many of you told us you take a screenshot of the same dashboard every week and paste it into Slack. Scheduled reports do that for you, and the numbers are never out of date.

To set one up, open a dashboard and select **Share** > **Schedule report**. Reports are free on every plan during October.

[Read how scheduled reports work](https://example.com/docs/scheduled-reports)

Thanks for reading,
Maya, Foo's product lead
