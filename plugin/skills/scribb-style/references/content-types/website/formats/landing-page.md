# Run your weekly team meeting in 15 minutes

Agenda is a meeting planner that builds the agenda from your team's updates. It works with the calendar you already use.

Your weekly meeting starts with everyone reading out status updates the rest could have read. Decisions get pushed to next week because time ran out.

A shared doc helps until nobody updates it. Agenda asks each person on Monday and does the sorting for you.

## Updates arrive before the meeting
Each person adds theirs on Monday. Agenda groups them by project.

## The meeting starts with what needs a decision
Anything marked "blocked" goes to the top.

"Our Tuesday meeting went from an hour to 15 minutes." Sam Ortiz, Head of Product at Brightline
