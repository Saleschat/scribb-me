# Why we built Ledgerly

Priya ran finance at a 40-person company. Every month she lost a week to reconciling transactions that matched perfectly well. She wanted that week back for her team, so she started Ledgerly with Tom, who had built the bank-feed tools she relied on.

We believe month-end should be a review. That's why Ledgerly shows you only what it couldn't match.
